# vfinance

Fetch Vietnam stock market data from public sources.

vfinance is a Python package designed to help analysts, researchers, and data teams
bring Vietnam market data into research, monitoring, and reporting workflows.

## Core features

| Feature                      | Business impact                                                                               |
|------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Vietnamese ticker quotes** | Fetch a ticker's quote to support market monitoring and pricing views.                        |
| **Ticker history**           | Fetch historical market data for trend analysis, investment research, and backtesting.        |
| **Market events**            | Fetch new listings, dividends, and other events to add context to market and ticker analysis. |

## Business value

- **Less manual data collection:** Spend more time analyzing market activity and
  less time gathering information from public sources.
- **Richer market context:** Consider quotes, historical data, and events together
  rather than viewing price movements in isolation.
- **Reusable research workflows:** Bring market data into dashboards, reports,
  and repeatable analysis processes.

Data availability and freshness depend on the underlying public sources.
